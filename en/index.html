Who is Nehm?

It's the story of a young man who had the privilege of joining Junior Chamber International very early. This experience revealed his leadership abilities through training in project management, administrative planning, and public speaking.

It is truly this crucible of international youth that shaped his journey and gave birth to a vision: purpose life — living with a clear and impactful purpose. From a young age, he grew alongside great entrepreneurs and local leaders, interacting with creative minds and committed leaders. Very quickly, he took up the torch, with the will to build something bigger than himself. Today, he has already invested in several fields through digital solutions: financial applications (fintech), stock management systems, tools for processing industries, solutions for foodtech and restaurant management. Multiple solutions have been developed and are actively managed today.

What I Offer

Training, coaching, and strategic partnership for entrepreneurs, teams, and organizations.

Business Strategy Coaching

I guide founders through the entire lifecycle of their enterprise — from ideation to scaling. My approach goes beyond products and services to address the full ecosystem: leadership development, team structure, financial management, market positioning, and long-term sustainability.

Youth Mentoring & Training

I've mentored over 100 young people across Paris, Benin, China, and other regions on leadership, productivity, time management, economics, and personal finances. Whether you're a student, early-stage founder, or young professional — I help you unlock your potential and build habits that last.

Keynote Speaking & Conferences

Available for conferences, panels, and workshops on topics including: African entrepreneurship, leadership in the digital age, technology and innovation, youth empowerment, and financial literacy. I bring real-world experience from the field to inspire and educate.

Corporate Advisory & Investor Readiness

I partner with companies seeking to attract investors, restructure operations, or develop tech-driven solutions. My background in software engineering and business strategy allows me to bridge the gap between technical teams and executive leadership.

Venture Building & Product Strategy

Engineering new business models, rapid prototyping (MVP), and guiding startups to Product-Market Fit. I combine my technical expertise with business acumen to help founders build scalable ventures that solve real African problems.
